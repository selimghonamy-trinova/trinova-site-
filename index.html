The gap in the market

Egyptian manufacturers stuck between manual processes and enterprise-grade systems.

An estimated 8,000–15,000 formal, mid-size industrial establishments in Egypt sit in an underserved middle tier — too complex to run on manual, disconnected processes, and too small to justify the cost and multi-year rollout of enterprise-grade systems built for a different scale of operation. Trinova is built specifically for that gap: tailored operational systems, scoped to the size of your floor, licensed annually rather than sold as a rigid, one-size-fits-all platform.
