The reality on the floor

You don't know how much work is sitting between cutting and packing.

In multi-stage garment production — cutting, sewing, finishing, packing — WIP at each stage is unknown until someone physically walks the floor. Fabric and trim reconciliation errors are common with high SKU counts. And once work goes to a subcontracted workshop, visibility drops to zero until the finished pieces come back.

Unknown WIP at each stage

How many pieces are in cutting vs. sewing vs. finishing? Nobody knows without counting by hand.

Fabric/trim reconciliation errors

High SKU and variant counts make material-to-order matching error-prone — mismatches surface late.

Subcontractor black box

Visibility drops to zero once work leaves for a subcontracted workshop — status is a phone call, not a system.
