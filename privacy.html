Privacy

A plain-language summary of what this website collects and why.

Last updated: August 2026
01

Information we collect

The contact form on this site collects the name, company, email address, sector, and message you choose to submit. That information is used only to respond to your inquiry — it is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

02

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers of any kind.

03

Third-party services

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. As a normal part of viewing any page, your browser requests those files directly from Google's servers, which may log standard request data under Google's own privacy policy — Trinova does not receive or control that data.

04

Hosting

This site is hosted on Vercel. Standard web server logs, such as IP address and browser type, may be collected by Vercel as part of normal hosting operations, independent of anything Trinova adds to the site itself.

Contact
