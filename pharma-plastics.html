The reality on the floor

You can't produce a full batch record on demand for an audit.

Pharma manufacturers face hard compliance requirements from the Egyptian EDA and GMP-aligned practices. Plastics-packaging manufacturers serving pharma or food clients inherit similar traceability demands. In both cases, raw material lot traceability has gaps, QC data lives in separate paper forms per shift, and producing a full batch record for an auditor means days of assembly, not a single query.

Disconnected QC data
