The reality on the floor

You can't trace a contaminated batch back to its raw material source.

Expired or near-expiry stock goes unnoticed until a physical count catches it — by then it's waste or, worse, it shipped. Manual shift-end paper logs reach management a day late. And when a customer or regulator asks "which batches used this ingredient?", the answer takes days of digging through binders, if it comes at all.

Expiry blind spots

Near-expiry and expired stock isn't flagged until the next physical count — FIFO is a policy on paper, not enforced in practice.

Traceability gaps

Can't trace a contaminated or recalled batch back to its raw material lot — a serious liability for exporters and HACCP compliance.

Delayed production data

Paper shift logs reach management a day late — decisions are made on yesterday's numbers, not today's reality.
