The reality on the floor

Stock discrepancies only surface at audit — after they've already cost you.

Picking errors cause wrong shipments and customer complaints. There's no live visibility into what's arriving versus what's committed to leave. For 3PL operators, inaccurate storage and handling tracking leads to imprecise client billing — a direct revenue leak.
