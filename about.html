A small team that stays close to the build.

Trinova is an industrial operational technology solutions company — not a software studio, not an AI solutions provider. We build the systems manufacturers run their floor on, and we stay in them.

Why "trinova"

Three pillars, built new.

"Tri" is production, inventory, and supply chain — the three pillars every manufacturer needs visibility into. "Nova" is a system built new around how your floor actually works, not retrofitted from a platform designed for someone else's process.

The team

Two people. Full accountability.

Founder & Managing Director

Owns strategy, client outreach, and the diagnostic conversation with every new manufacturer — the person you talk to before anything gets built.

Technical Director

Leads the technical build full-time, working directly with your team to get the system to match the floor exactly.

Delivery capacity scales per project through hired contractors — so the core team stays small and close to every build, while capacity flexes to the size of the job.

How we work

Diagnose first. Build second. Stay third.

Diagnose

We start with a conversation about your floor, not a product pitch. The entry point is whatever hurts most.

Build

Delivery is measured in weeks, not quarters — a tailored system, not a configured template.

Support

Grow

Add modules as your needs grow. Every module works standalone and connects with the others.
