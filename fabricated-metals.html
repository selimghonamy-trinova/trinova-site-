The reality on the floor

You don't know a job's real cost until it's already shipped.

In job-shop and make-to-order metal fabrication, every job is different — its own materials, its own routing, its own machine time. That makes it almost impossible to track by spreadsheet. Most shops find out whether a job was profitable weeks after it's out the door, if they find out at all. Meanwhile, unplanned equipment downtime eats margin with no record of why the line stopped, and raw material — sheet metal, bar stock, alloys — goes untracked between the storeroom and the shop floor until the next stock-take contradicts the paperwork.
